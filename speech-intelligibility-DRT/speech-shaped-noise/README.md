# Speech-Shaped Noise for Intelligibility Tests

We generated speech-shaped noise, which may be used to increase the difficulty of intelligibility tests and force listeners to focus rely on critical perceptual cues. 

The speech-shaped noise was generated from Crowdsourced high-quality UK and Ireland English Dialect speech subset of OpenSLR corpus (https://openslr.org/83), published under [CC BY-SA 4.0 license](https://openslr.trmal.net/resources/83/LICENSE), at a sampling rate of 48 kHz. Long-term-average short-term spectra were computed using the Welch's method followed by sixth-octave band smoothing. We also provide downsampled versions at 24 and 16 kHz. 

The SSN file are made publicly available here for the benefit of the wider research community under CC BY-SA 4.0 license (https://github.com/cisco/multilingual-speech-testing/blob/main/LICENSE.txt).
